TagMacro turns cheap NFC stickers into buttons. Tap a tag with your phone and it runs a list of actions you chose: silence the phone at bedtime, tell someone you got home, start a timer, open your maps app in the car, or note when you fed the cat.

It is also a complete tag tool: read any tag, and write links, Wi-Fi details, contact cards and more that work on any phone, with no app needed.

<b>Private by design</b>
<ul>
<li>No internet permission. Android does not let TagMacro open a network connection at all.</li>
<li>No ads, no trackers, no analytics, and no libraries beyond the Android framework and Kotlin's standard library.</li>
<li>Tags written by TagMacro hold only a random code, never your messages or contacts.</li>
<li>Every permission is optional and only requested for the actions that need it.</li>
</ul>

<b>Actions</b>
<ul>
<li>Messages and calls: send an SMS, call a number, send a Signal or WhatsApp message, share live location on WhatsApp</li>
<li>Sound: ringer mode, Do Not Disturb, volumes, play/pause/next</li>
<li>Screen and device: brightness, screen timeout, auto-rotate, flashlight, lock screen, Wi-Fi/NFC/volume panels</li>
<li>Apps and places: open an app, open a link, get directions</li>
<li>Time: alarms, timers, waits</li>
<li>Feedback and notes: on-screen message, speech, vibration, clipboard, and a journal that records when things happen</li>
<li>Run another macro</li>
</ul>
Messages can include the time, date, battery level or a map link to where you are.

<b>Triggers and conditions</b>
<ul>
<li>A tag written by TagMacro, any tag or card by its serial number, or a home screen shortcut</li>
<li>Ask before running, cooldowns, time-of-day windows and days of the week</li>
</ul>

<b>Tag tools</b>
<ul>
<li>Read: chip type, capacity, lock state, every record, and which macro a tag runs</li>
<li>Write: links, Wi-Fi networks, contact cards, map locations, phone numbers, SMS, email, text, app launchers</li>
<li>Erase, format and permanently lock tags</li>
</ul>

<b>Also</b>
Ready-made templates, a run log that shows each step, journal export to CSV, and full backup and restore.

<b>Good to know</b>
Signal, WhatsApp and lock-screen actions use an accessibility service that only receives events from Signal and WhatsApp and only acts while a macro is running. WhatsApp's terms forbid automated messaging, so use those actions at your own risk. Android does not allow apps to switch Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, mobile data or airplane mode on or off.
